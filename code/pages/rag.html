RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Covers roadmap sections on RAG fundamentals, chunking, advanced retrieval patterns, and evaluation.

RAG Fundamentals and Architecture

  1. Documents -> loader -> chunking -> embeddings -> vector DB
  2. Retriever gathers relevant chunks
  3. Context is grounded in prompt for LLM answer generation

What to Study

  • RAG architecture components rag-arch
  • Document ingestion and chunking flow rag-ingestion
  • Context construction and grounding rag-context-construction
  • Common causes of poor answers rag-failure-modes

Interview Questions

  • What is RAG and why not send full documents directly to the model?
  • RAG vs fine-tuning: when do you pick each?
  • What are the major components that break in production RAG?

Chunking and Embeddings

What to Study

  • Chunking styles: fixed, recursive, semantic, parent-child rag-chunk-fixed
  • Chunk overlap and its retrieval tradeoffs rag-chunk-overlap
  • Embedding models: BGE, E5, OpenAI, Sentence Transformers rag-embed-models
  • Cosine vs dot product vs Euclidean distance rag-similarity-metrics

Interview Questions

  • How do you choose chunk size for legal docs vs source code?
  • Why can chunks that are too small or too large both degrade quality?
  • How do you choose an embedding model for a production domain?

Advanced Retrieval and Reranking

  1. Dense + sparse/hybrid retrieval for better candidate recall
  2. Query rewriting/expansion/decomposition to improve intent match
  3. Rerank top candidates (cross-encoder, relevance scoring)
  4. Send only strongest grounded context to the LLM

What to Study

  • Hybrid retrieval (dense + sparse/BM25) rag-hybrid
  • Query rewriting and multi-query retrieval rag-query-rewrite
  • Reranking with cross-encoders rag-rerank
  • Graph/agentic/corrective/adaptive RAG patterns rag-advanced-architectures

Interview Questions

  • When is reranking mandatory instead of optional?
  • How does query rewriting improve retrieval recall in enterprise search?
  • How would you design a multi-hop RAG pipeline?
